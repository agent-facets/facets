---
title: General Availability
description: Plans and criteria for general availability
---

General availability begins when the beta commitments and compatibility criteria below are met. Every npm package Agent Facets publishes, including the CLI platform packages, reaches v1.0.0 together.

## Access at GA

- **Public facets.** Stay free to publish, discover, and install.
- **Personal private facets.** Move to a paid plan. Anything you published privately while it was free stays private and accessible at no cost.
- **Private facets for teams.** Become self-service instead of activated by request.
- **Enterprise support.** Available for large enterprise customers.

## GA criteria

- **Beta features.** Every feature from the open beta is complete and stable.
- **Published packages.** All published Agent Facets packages reach v1.0.0 together.
- **Format compatibility.** The archive format, `facets.json`, and `facets.lock` remain [independent version axes](/specification/install#version-axes). Each reaches v1 with the published packages, as does the [adapter SDK API](/reference/adapter-sdk) on its own axis.
- **Specification completeness.** The [specification](/specification) fully describes the product, locks to v1, and is versioned from then on.

Releases are logged in the [changelog](/changelog).
